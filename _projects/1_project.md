## Overview

**Status:** Ongoing master's thesis research

This research examines the perceptual-cognitive aspects of population emergency evacuation in a virtual-reality environment in the presence of an AI assistant.

**Research team:** Seyed Mahdi Ebrahimi Mousavi, Dr. Navid Khademi (supervisor), and Dr. Keyvan Aghabeyk (thesis advisor).

The project is situated at the intersection of human behavior, transportation, virtual reality, and AI-assisted decision support. Further methodological details and findings will be added as the research develops.
